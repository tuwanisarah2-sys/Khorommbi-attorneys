Meet Our Team

Matamela Khorommbi

Director & Founder

The Director of Khorommbi K Attorneys Inc grew up in a small South African village, where the distance between people and justice was measured in more than kilometres. That early proximity to inequity became a quiet, durable conviction that the law, practised well, can move a country forward.

Years later, that conviction is the spine of this firm. Every brief is approached with the same belief: that historical injustice is best answered by present, deliberate work, and that meaningful transformation begins one client, one matter, at a time.

Carolynn Kistadoo

Candidate Attorney LLB LLM

Carolynn graduated cum laude with her LLB degree in 2022 from Varsity College. During her semester breaks, she completed vacation work at various law firms and organizations which exposed her to property, family and environmental law. In addition to her vacation work, Carolynn volunteered with Probono.Org at Constitution Hill, where she completed 40 hours of community service.

Driven by her passion for the environment, Carolynn pursued a master's degree in marine and environmental law, further strengthening her commitment to sustainable legal practices and environmental justice.

Nozuko Mbeki
