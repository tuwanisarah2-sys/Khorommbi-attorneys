Meet Our Team

"

Great teams are built on trust, shared vision, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Matamela Khorommbi

Director & Founder

The Director of Khorommbi Attorneys Inc grew up in a small South African village, where the distance between people and justice was measured in more than kilometres. That early proximity to inequity became a quiet, durable conviction that the law, practised well, can move a country forward.

Years later, that conviction is the spine of this firm. Every brief is approached with the same belief: that historical injustice is best answered by present, deliberate work, and that meaningful transformation begins one client, one matter, at a time.

Jo-Anne Janse van Rensburg

Candidate Attorney

Jo-Anne is a dedicated and passionate Candidate Attorney with a strong commitment to providing professional, efficient, and client-focused legal services. She is passionate about the law and continuously developing her knowledge and practical experience across various areas of legal practice.

Her role involves assisting with legal research, drafting legal documents, consulting with clients, preparing matters for court, and appearing in court where authorised. Jo-Anne is committed to upholding the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and diligence while working towards achieving the best possible outcomes for clients.

Nozuko Petrose

Candidate Attorney LLB (UNISA)

Nozuko is a motivated Candidate Attorney with a strong sense of purpose, resilience, and commitment to excellence. She holds an LLB from the University of South Africa (UNISA) and a Higher Certificate in Criminal Justice from Nelson Mandela University. Her journey has shaped her into a dependable, reflective, and deeply invested legal professional dedicated to doing meaningful work.

Nozuko thrives in environments that require responsibility, precision, and compassion. Her strengths include legal research, drafting, organisation, and effective communication with clients and colleagues. She values integrity, accountability, and continuous learning, and is building a career grounded in service, professionalism, and growth—seeking opportunities to add value while developing into a well-rounded legal practitioner.
